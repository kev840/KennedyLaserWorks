A clear, collaborative process

From first thought to finished piece.

You do not need a production-ready design to begin. Share the purpose, wording, inspiration, or occasion, and the practical details can be shaped together before creation starts.

  1. IdeaShare what you want to make, who it is for, and why it matters.
  2. SketchEstablish the rough layout, wording, size, style, and useful references.
  3. DesignRefine the direction around the agreed project details and production needs.
  4. ApprovalReview the design proof, scope, timing, and quote. Creation begins only after the agreed direction is approved.
  5. CreationYour piece is cut or engraved, assembled where needed, and finished by hand.
  6. DeliveryArrange advance pickup in Budd Lake or eligible nationwide shipping.
