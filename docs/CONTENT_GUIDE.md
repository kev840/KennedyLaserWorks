# Content Guide

## Voice

Write with warmth, confidence, and restraint. Emphasize care, personalization, and the made-to-order process. Avoid marketplace clichés, exaggerated luxury language, urgency tricks, and unsupported superlatives.

## Verified facts

- Kennedy Laser Works is based in Budd Lake, New Jersey.
- Products may be designed, laser engraved or cut, assembled, painted, and finished with care.
- Pieces are made to order and custom work is welcomed.
- Local pickup is available by arrangement and nationwide shipping is available.
- Budd Lake customers can save 10% when they contact Kennedy Laser Works before ordering to arrange pickup and the discount.

## Do not publish without evidence

- Years in business, sales totals, awards, certifications, staff, or sustainability claims
- Customer testimonials or “best seller” labels
- Product prices, availability, or Etsy URLs not checked against current listings
- A public telephone number

## Images

Use actual finished-work photography wherever available. Write alt text that describes the object and relevant craftsmanship without repeating surrounding copy. Decorative logos should use empty alt text when the adjacent brand name provides the same information.

## Seasonal content

Update the configuration in `js/seasonal.js`. Begin promotion early enough for made-to-order lead times, but keep all collections permanently available through the catalog.
