Behind every piece

Craftsmanship with a human touch.

I’m the owner and craftsman behind Kennedy Laser Works. I create custom laser-engraved signs, personalized gifts, plaques, and one-of-a-kind pieces using a combination of laser technology and traditional woodworking techniques.

From designing and engraving to sanding, painting, and finishing, I take pride in making each piece something you’ll be proud to give or display.

Meet Kevin

Made carefully, one order at a time.

I create custom, made-to-order pieces at Kennedy Laser Works, combining laser engraving with traditional woodworking techniques. From the initial design to the final finish, I personally handle each project with attention to detail.

Whether you have a finished design, a rough idea, or just an occasion in mind, I’ll work with you to create something special.

My goal is simple: to create something personal, meaningful, and made just for you.

Finished projects can be shipped nationwide, and local pickup is available by arrangement in Budd Lake.
