Privacy policy

How this static website handles information.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Information you choose to send

This website does not operate its own customer database or checkout. When you use the custom-project form, the site prepares a message in your email application. Your information is sent only when you choose to send that email.

Third-party services

Purchases and Etsy messages are handled by Etsy and are subject to Etsy’s own privacy practices. Facebook interactions are handled by Facebook. Your email provider handles messages sent to Kennedy Laser Works.

Website analytics and cookies

This version of the website does not include analytics, advertising pixels, or a custom cookie system. If those services are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before deployment.

Information use

Information sent to Kennedy Laser Works may be used to answer questions, prepare quotes, discuss custom work, arrange pickup or shipping, and provide related customer service.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to kennedylaserworks@gmail.com.
